Contact us

For Block Mirage support and privacy requests, email karagozpasha24@gmail.com.

Use Block Mirage — Support for help, or Block Mirage — Privacy request for privacy enquiries.

Describe the issue

Include your platform, device model, operating-system and app versions, when the issue occurs, and any steps to reproduce it. Screenshots are optional.

You do not need to send passwords, identity documents, card details or advertising identifiers. Redact personal information from screenshots. For an ad issue, tell us whether it was rewarded or interstitial and whether it appeared.

Privacy or deletion

The privacy policy explains data practices. The deletion guide explains how to manage local saves, ad choices and externally shared copies separately.

Keep support requests private

Use the email address for support or privacy enquiries. Do not post identity information or private requests on public GitHub pages. The email button opens your email app; it is not a website submission form.
