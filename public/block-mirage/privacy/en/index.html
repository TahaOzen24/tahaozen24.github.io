1. About this policy and the developer

This policy covers Block Mirage, a mobile game developed by KaragozPasha Studio, and this privacy and support website. For privacy enquiries, contact karagozpasha24@gmail.com.

The advertising descriptions currently apply to Google AdMob in the Android version. If different services are added on other platforms, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated.

2. Game data stored on your device

The board and saved run, scores, personal best, adventure progress, daily results, statistics, appearance preferences and settings such as sound and haptics are kept in app-specific device storage. A local guest profile identifier and local backup files support save integrity and continuity.

These records are not uploaded to a developer-operated game server. Playing does not require an account, password, name or email address. Depending on your operating-system settings, app data may be included in device backups; this is not a developer-operated cloud-save service.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Android version uses Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial ads. The advertising service may process an IP address and inferred general location, advertising/device and applicable account-related identifiers, app/ad interactions, and technical diagnostic information.

Purposes may include ad delivery and measurement, service analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google uses TLS for advertising-data transmission. We do not separately send local board saves or personal-best files to the advertising provider.

Personalized, non-personalized or limited ads depend on your region, choices and the provider’s supported options. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

Where required, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents a privacy/consent message. Ad requests start according to the permission status reported by that system.

When required, the game displays Settings → Other settings → Ad privacy (currently labelled “Ayarlar → Diğer ayarlar → Reklam gizliliği” in Turkish). You can reopen your choices there. Changes affect current advertising options and do not reverse prior lawful processing. Rejecting personalization does not necessarily remove all ads.

You can manage your advertising identifier in Android’s ad-privacy settings. Google account options are also available through My Ad Center.

5. Unity and technical operation

The game runs on the Unity engine. Basic technical/usage features, depending on the engine version and platform, may process device, operating-system, app-version and runtime-performance information. We do not operate a separate cloud player account or online player-analytics system; separate Unity Analytics gameplay-event tracking and Unity Cloud Diagnostics reporting are not enabled in the current configuration.

For Unity’s own technology practices, see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

6. Score images and sharing you initiate

A score image is generated on your device when you request one. It may contain your score, game mode and related game results. Only if you choose to share it is it passed to the app you select through the operating-system share sheet. The game does not automatically upload it to a social network.

Copies you send elsewhere are subject to the receiving app’s privacy and retention practices.

7. Support correspondence

If you email us, your email address, message and any device details or screenshots you voluntarily attach are processed to respond to your request. Support email uses Gmail; Google’s policies apply to the email service’s processing.

Please do not send passwords, banking/card information, identity documents or unnecessary personal data. Redact information about other people from screenshots.

8. Purposes and legal grounds

Local records provide the game and preserve progress; support data is used to respond to your request; limited technical information supports secure and reliable operation. Advertising purposes are described above.

Depending on the processing, applicable grounds may include providing the service you request, legitimate interests in security and support, legal obligations, and consent where required. You can withdraw consent-based choices using the relevant preference tools.

9. Providers and international processing

Google (AdMob, UMP and support email), Unity (engine/technical services), and GitHub (website hosting) provide the functions described here. Their infrastructure may process data outside your country; their policies describe their processing terms, retention practices and applicable transfer safeguards.

We do not sell local game saves to advertisers. The advertising provider’s processing and sharing described above must be considered separately from that statement. Limited disclosure may also occur for applicable legal obligations or protection of rights.

10. Retention and deletion

Local game data may remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Device/operating-system backups and externally shared files are managed separately. Support correspondence is retained only while needed to handle the request, necessary follow-up and applicable legal retention needs; it is then deleted or made no longer identifiable.

Advertising and hosting providers retain their own records under their policies. Clearing app data does not automatically erase those records. See the data deletion and choices guide for practical steps.

11. Security

Local files are kept in app-specific storage subject to operating-system access controls. This website uses HTTPS. Third-party services have their own security measures. No device, storage or transmission method can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

12. Children’s privacy

Block Mirage is not designed as a child-specific application. We do not knowingly ask children for names, school details, addresses or contact information. A parent or guardian who believes a child’s personal information has been sent to us can contact us for review and any deletion required by applicable obligations.

The target audience and content rating displayed in the store should also be considered.

13. This website

We do not place ads, visitor-analytics tools, external fonts or tracking cookies on this website. It has no data-submission form or user login; email links open your own email application.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation under its own policy. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

14. Your rights and contact

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to information/access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. You may also have a right to contact the competent data-protection authority.

Send requests about data controlled by the developer to karagozpasha24@gmail.com. For records that remain on your device, follow the deletion guide; for data processed directly by Google, Unity or GitHub, you may also need to use that provider’s tools.

Material changes to data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date and, where required, additional in-app information or consent.
