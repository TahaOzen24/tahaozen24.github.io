No game account is required

Block Mirage does not create a developer-operated online account. There is no game account to close or cloud-save profile on our server to delete. Local saves, advertising-provider data and support emails are stored separately.

1. Clear Android game data

  1. Open your phone’s Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → Block Mirage → Storage.
  3. Choose Clear data / Clear storage. Names vary by device.

This can permanently remove progress, personal bests, saved games and preferences. It cannot be undone. Uninstalling normally removes local app data too, but an operating-system backup may restore it on reinstallation.

2. Manage backups and shared images separately

Check your device’s cloud-backup settings. Delete score images you saved or shared from their respective locations. Clearing app data alone does not delete copies held by recipients or sharing services.

3. Change advertising choices

When required, reopen preferences through Settings → Other settings → Ad privacy (Turkish: “Ayarlar → Diğer ayarlar → Reklam gizliliği”). You can also use Android’s ad-privacy settings and My Ad Center.

These controls do not mean that all historical third-party records are automatically deleted. You may need to use the relevant provider’s privacy tools for records held by Google or Unity.

4. Send a request to the developer

For support correspondence or other data controlled by the developer, email karagozpasha24@gmail.com. Explain which interaction your request concerns and what you would like us to do. Do not attach unnecessary sensitive documents.

We cannot remotely access or erase saves that remain on your device. Records subject to applicable retention obligations may be kept for a limited period for that reason; requests are assessed within the appropriate scope.
